# Sri Lanka Prediction Market Research

This project presents a research-based concept for a prediction market platform tailored to Sri Lanka.

## Overview
The project explores prediction markets for real-world events such as:
- Cost of living changes
- Fuel and electricity prices
- Education outcomes
- Employment trends

## Legal & Market Analysis
Includes research on:
- Sri Lankan gambling laws
- Betting and Gaming Levy Act
- Gambling Regulatory Authority Act (2025)

## Platform Concept
- YES/NO and multi-outcome prediction system
- Player vs Player model
- Charity-based monetization
- Secure wallet and payment integration
